THE AADVIDYA EXPERIENCE

Curiosity in science.
Humanity in practice.

AADVIDYA is envisioned as a gathering for aspiring healthcare professionals to exchange ideas, test their skills, celebrate creativity, and build meaningful connections beyond the classroom.

As MNR Medical College marks a milestone of excellence, AADVIDYA 26 is an opportunity to honour the journey so far and look ahead to a future shaped by compassionate, evidence-led healthcare.

✓Learn togetherAcademic exchange and hands-on discovery.
✓Compete with purposeChallenge your skills in a supportive spirit.
